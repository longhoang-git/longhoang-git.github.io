About

I am an electrical engineer with five years of experience across renewable energy consultancy and power-system research, specialising in HV cable systems, substation concepts and power-system studies, with a working knowledge of energy markets and policy.

At OWC – an Aqualis Company, I have delivered electrical design, technical due diligence and route-to-market studies for 20+ offshore and onshore wind, solar PV, BESS and interconnector projects from 50 MW to 2 GW, led three electrical work packages, one rated 9/10 by the client, and developed an inter-array cable layout optimiser that shortens cable length by 10–30%. My research has covered microgrid control, hydrogen systems and national hydrogen policy at HUST, and now early-stage grid-connection costs at UCL, where I am studying MSc Power Systems Engineering while continuing to advise OWC on MV/HV design and power-system studies.
