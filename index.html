About

I am an electrical engineer with five years of experience across renewable energy consultancy and power-system research, specialising in HV cable systems, substation concepts and power-system studies.

At OWC – an Aqualis Company, I have delivered electrical design for 20+ offshore wind, solar PV, BESS and interconnector projects from 50 MW to 2 GW, led three electrical work packages, one rated 9/10 by the client, and developed an inter-array cable layout optimiser that shortens cable length by 10–30%. Alongside my MSc in Power Systems Engineering at UCL, I continue to advise OWC on HV and LV design, technical due diligence and power-system studies.
